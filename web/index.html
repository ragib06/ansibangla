    Bijoy to Unicode, the whole file at once

    Most Bijoy converters take text pasted into a box. This one takes the whole PDF or Word file typed in SutonnyMJ or another Bijoy font and gives back Unicode Bangla with the same pages, headings, pictures and formatting, ready to search, copy and edit.

    Questions

    Why does text from my Bijoy PDF come out as gibberish?

    Bijoy (ANSI) fonts such as SutonnyMJ draw Bangla letters on top of English key codes, so the file really stores text like wbwØ©avq. It only looks right in that font, and copying, searching or opening it elsewhere shows the codes. Unicode stores the real letters (নির্দ্বিধায়), so the text works everywhere.

    বিজয় পিডিএফ থেকে লেখা কপি করলে হিজিবিজি আসে কেন?

    বিজয়ের সুতন্বী এমজে-র মতো ফন্ট ইংরেজি অক্ষরের জায়গায় বাংলা দেখায়, তাই ফাইলে আসলে ইংরেজি কোড থাকে। ইউনিকোডে রূপান্তর করলে আসল বাংলা অক্ষর পাওয়া যায়, যা যেকোনো জায়গায় কপি, সার্চ ও এডিট করা যায়।

    Which files can I convert?

    PDFs and Word .docx files typed in SutonnyMJ and other Bijoy fonts. Save an older .doc file as .docx in Word first. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) have no text to convert.

    What do I get back?

    A Unicode PDF with the same page layout, a Word document with the original formatting, an EPUB e-book, or Markdown. Pick any combination.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The converter runs inside your browser, so your files never leave your device. After the first visit it loads from your browser's cache.
